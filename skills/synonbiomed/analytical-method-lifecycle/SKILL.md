# Analytical Method Lifecycle

Match procedure performance to the decision it supports.

## Workflow

1. Define the analytical target profile, reportable result, matrix, range, specification or decision limit, and required uncertainty.
2. Select technology and sample preparation based on analyte, matrix, interference, degradation, and operational constraints.
3. Develop the method using knowledge and risk assessment; identify critical method parameters and control ranges.
4. Demonstrate specificity or selectivity, accuracy, precision, range, response, detection or quantitation capability, and robustness as appropriate to intended use.
5. Predefine protocols, acceptance criteria, calculations, data handling, deviations, and change rules.
6. Plan qualification versus validation by development phase and regulatory purpose.
7. Execute transfer or verification with site, instrument, reagent, software, and analyst controls.
8. Monitor lifecycle performance and investigate shifts, OOS/OOT results, and method changes.

## Deliver

Produce the analytical target profile, development report outline, validation or qualification protocol, characteristics matrix, transfer plan, lifecycle monitoring plan, and gap assessment.

Do not apply every validation characteristic mechanically. Do not report a method as validated without approved protocol execution and traceable data.

## Source authorities

Use current [ICH Q2(R2) and Q14 guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q2r2-validation-analytical-procedures), [ICH Quality guidance](https://admin.ich.org/page/quality-guidelines), and ICH M10 for bioanalytical methods.
