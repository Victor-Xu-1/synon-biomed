UCSC Genome Browser chromosome-size metadata

The four *.chrom.sizes files in this directory are two-column sequence-name
and sequence-length tables retrieved from the UCSC Genome Browser download
service on 2026-07-29. They contain no genomic sequence, software binary, or
annotation track. Exact source URLs, assembly accessions, byte counts, and
checksums are recorded in PROVENANCE.json.

UCSC states that sequence and annotation data displayed in the Genome Browser
are freely available for any use subject to the conditions and attribution
requirements at https://genome.ucsc.edu/conditions.html. Please credit the
UCSC Genome Browser Group and the Genome Reference Consortium / NCBI assembly
contributors when these data are used in a publication or web page.

These files are provided for research visualization. They are not a clinical
reference and must not be used by themselves to make treatment decisions.
