# Postmarketing Lifecycle Management

Coordinate safety, quality, regulatory, medical, and supply changes after approval.

## Workflow

1. Inventory approvals, labels, commitments, conditions, risk-management measures, specifications, sites, markets, and regulatory correspondence.
2. Build the launch and post-launch obligation calendar: safety reports, periodic reports, postmarketing studies, renewals, inspections, quality reviews, and commitments.
3. Define signal detection, real-world evidence, utilization, medication-error, effectiveness, and benefit-risk monitoring.
4. Govern label changes, new indications, new strengths, formulations, routes, devices, sites, suppliers, process changes, and comparability.
5. Classify supplements or variations by jurisdiction using current authority rules.
6. Link quality performance, stability, continued process verification, complaints, deviations, recalls, shortages, and CAPA.
7. Track commitments and effectiveness of risk-minimization actions.
8. Define escalation, health-authority communication, change control, decision rights, and archival evidence.

## Deliver

Produce a postmarketing obligation register, lifecycle calendar, change-impact matrix, safety and quality dashboard design, commitment tracker, and escalation plan.

Do not assume approval in one jurisdiction permits the same change elsewhere. Verify current label, application, and authority rules before action.

## Source authorities

Use the FDA [post-market safety monitoring framework](https://www.fda.gov/patients/drug-development-process/step-5-fda-post-market-drug-safety-monitoring), [ICH Q12 lifecycle management](https://admin.ich.org/page/quality-guidelines), current ICH E2 guidance, and applicable EMA GVP or regional post-authorization requirements.
