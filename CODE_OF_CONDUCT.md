# Code of conduct

Synon Biomed welcomes people with different scientific, engineering, and lived
experiences. Our community should make it possible to ask questions, challenge
an interpretation, and improve a result without attacking the person behind it.

本项目欢迎不同背景的参与者。可以严格质疑代码、观点和证据，但请尊重具体的人，
保护他人的隐私与未公开研究。

## How we work together

- Be respectful. Give newcomers enough context to participate and consider
  language and accessibility needs.
- Critique the claim, code, or evidence. Explain what is wrong and how someone
  could verify or improve it; do not make personal attacks.
- Assume good faith while taking reported harm seriously. Accept corrections
  and give others room to correct mistakes.
- Protect private data, credentials, unpublished research, and confidential
  business information, including in logs, screenshots, and examples.
- Respect the repository's licenses, [security policy](SECURITY.md), and
  maintainer moderation decisions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets,
and disruptive bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

## Where this applies

This code applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project-managed community spaces. It also applies when someone is
explicitly representing the project in a public space.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the [repository owner](https://github.com/Victor-Xu-1) through a private
contact route listed on their public profile. If no private route is listed,
request one without posting the incident, identities, or sensitive evidence
publicly. Include the relevant links, what happened, and any immediate concern
once a private route is established.

For a software vulnerability, use the separate reporting process in
[Security](SECURITY.md). A vulnerability advisory is not the default channel
for interpersonal conduct concerns.

行为问题请先联系仓库所有者并确认私密沟通渠道，不要在公开 Issue 中披露当事人信息；
软件漏洞则按安全政策报告。

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask for clarification or correction, moderate or remove
content, and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors
or the project. The response should reflect the severity and repetition of
the behavior. Reporting a concern is not permission to publish someone else's
private information.
