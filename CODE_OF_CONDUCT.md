# Code of conduct

We want a respectful, evidence-based project community.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome different backgrounds and accessibility needs;
- protect private data, credentials, unpublished research, and other sensitive
  material; and
- follow the repository licenses, security policy, and maintainer decisions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of
secrets, and disruptive bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may clarify, moderate, or remove
content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors
or the project.
