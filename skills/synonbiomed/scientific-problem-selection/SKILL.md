# Scientific Problem Selection

Turn an appealing idea into a falsifiable, staged research decision.

## Provenance

Adapted from an Apache-2.0 scientific workflow. Complete attribution and license text are retained in `LICENSE.txt` and `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`. The framework cites Fischbach and Walsh, Cell (2024).

## Choose an entry

Use one path:

1. Develop a new project idea.
2. Diagnose a current project problem.
3. Answer a strategic research question.

Ask for the goal, current evidence, major assumptions, constraints, value if successful, and the decision that must be made.

## Workflow

Load only the relevant files under `references/`:

- intuition pumps and inversion for idea generation;
- risk assessment and adversity planning for failure analysis;
- optimization function and parameter strategy for prioritization;
- decision tree for sequencing tests;
- integration synthesis and meta-framework for final planning.

Retrieve literature and precedent before scoring novelty or feasibility. Separate technical, biological, translational, operational, resource, timing, and adoption risks. Move high-risk assumptions earlier and design the smallest experiment that materially changes the decision.

## Deliver

Produce a concise project thesis, assumption register, evidence map, risk matrix, decision tree, go/no-go criteria, highest-information experiments, resource and timing implications, and alternative paths.

Do not turn subjective scores into false precision. Record who owns each judgment, the evidence date, uncertainty, and what result would reverse the recommendation.
