# Pharmacovigilance and Risk Management

Build a lifecycle safety system with traceable decisions and protected patient data.

## Workflow

1. Define product, populations, lifecycle phase, regions, safety specification, known and potential risks, missing information, and class effects.
2. Map case intake, triage, follow-up, seriousness, expectedness, causality, coding, duplicate management, quality control, and expedited reporting.
3. Define clinical-trial and post-market safety governance, medical review, safety committees, reconciliation, and vendor oversight.
4. Plan signal detection, validation, prioritization, assessment, recommendation, communication, and closure with audit trails.
5. Define periodic and aggregate reports, data locks, analyses, literature surveillance, and benefit-risk evaluation.
6. Build pharmacovigilance and risk-minimization activities with objectives, measures, effectiveness metrics, and update triggers.
7. Address safety-system quality, training, inspection readiness, deviations, CAPA, and records retention.

## Deliver

Produce a safety specification, PV process map, reporting calendar, signal-management plan, RMP outline, risk-minimization effectiveness plan, governance matrix, and gaps.

Do not process identifiable patient data outside approved systems. Do not infer causality or regulatory reportability from incomplete information; route decisions to qualified safety professionals.

## Source authorities

Use current [ICH E2 clinical safety guidance](https://admin.ich.org/page/efficacy-guidelines), [EMA Good Pharmacovigilance Practices](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/post-authorisation/pharmacovigilance-post-authorisation/good-pharmacovigilance-practices-gvp), and applicable FDA or regional reporting requirements.
